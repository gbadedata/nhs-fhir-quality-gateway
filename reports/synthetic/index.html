How to interpret this dashboard

A message can pass the HL7 validator and still be wrong in ways that matter to a clinician: an Observation linked to another patient's Encounter, or a reading timed outside the visit it belongs to. Those are the rules checked here. Passing both layers is a prerequisite for trusting the data, not evidence of clinical accuracy.

Visit the README for the schema contract, risk log, testing instructions and an explanation of how formal NHS clinical risk management differs from unit tests.
